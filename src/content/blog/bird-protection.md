Protective plant grid designed to shield newly planted seedlings from birds.
Place over individual pots or seed trays to create a physical barrier while
still allowing sunlight, rain, and airflow to reach young plants.

Print with top and bottom shell at zero, with the infill pattern you prefer (I
use honeycomb).
